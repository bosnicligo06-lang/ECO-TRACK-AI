Greater Chennai Corporation Segregation Guidelines

Mandatory 3-way segregation protocols under GCC Solid Waste Management Bylaws.

🟢 Wet Organic Waste

  • Cooked and uncooked food leftovers
  • Fruit peels, vegetable trimmings, seeds
  • Garden leaves, coconut shells, flowers
  • Coffee grounds and tea leaves

🔵 Dry Recyclable Waste

  • Paper, cardboard, newspapers, cartons
  • Plastic bottles, containers, clean poly bags
  • Metal cans, foil containers, scrap metal
  • Glass bottles, jars, unbroken glassware

🔴 Sanitary & Hazardous Waste

  • Sanitary napkins, diapers, bandages
  • Expired medications and blister packs
  • Paints, pesticides, thinners, batteries
  • Fluorescent tubes and electronic waste
